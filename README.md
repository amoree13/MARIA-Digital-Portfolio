# 🎨 ACTIVITY 1: PRESENTATION DESIGN PRINCIPLES

## Arrupe Hall Booth 7 Promotional Design

For this activity, I created a promotional design for **Arrupe Hall Booth 7**.

The main message of the design is:

> **FREE COFFEE.**
>
> **NO CATCH, JUST CAFFEINE AND GOOD VIBES.**
>
> **FIRST COME, FIRST SERVED.**

## 💡 Concept and Creative Process

The concept was created to immediately capture the attention of students and encourage them to visit Booth 7 at Arrupe Hall.

I used a photograph of Arrupe Hall as the background to establish a direct connection between the promotional material and its location. A dark blue overlay was placed over the image to improve the visibility and readability of the text.

The dominant use of blue was intentional. As an Atenean, I initially wanted to establish a visual identity inspired by the color blue and its strong association with the Ateneo community.

Large and bold typography was used to establish **visual hierarchy** and make the main message immediately noticeable. The words **"ARRUPE HALL"** and **"BOOTH 7"** were emphasized to clearly communicate the location.

A coffee illustration was also included to support the theme and make the design more visually engaging.

# 📊 ACTIVITY 3: SOCIAL MEDIA INFOGRAPHICS AND MINI PROJECT DOCUMENTATION

## Coping with Financial Blind Spots in Student Spending

For this activity, I created an infographic titled:

### **Coping with Financial Blind Spots in Student Spending**

The infographic focuses on the financial challenges experienced by students, particularly small and frequent expenses that may go unnoticed but can eventually contribute to overspending.

## 💡 Concept

The concept was developed around the idea that students often spend money on small purchases such as:

- Food
- Transportation
- School projects
- Other daily expenses

These expenses may seem small individually, but they can accumulate over time.

Another challenge presented in the infographic is that student spending can be divided among different payment methods, including:

- Cash
- E-wallets
- Bank transfers

Because transactions can be scattered across different platforms, it may become difficult for students to see the complete picture of their spending.

## 🔢 By the Numbers

The infographic presents supporting information and statistics related to cashless transactions, financial planning, and access to financial tools.

These statistics were included to provide context and support the identified problem.

## 💻 Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is a budgeting and expense-tracking system that can help students monitor their spending.

The proposed system can:

- Log transactions manually
- Capture expenses from e-wallets and banks
- Use built-in formulas to calculate totals
- Track budget limits
- Provide forecasting
- Provide alerts and weekly summaries

## ⚙️ How It Works

The proposed solution follows these steps:

### 1. RECORD
Log expenses from cash, e-wallets, bank transfers, or uploaded receipts.

### 2. CATEGORIZE
Sort transactions into categories such as food, transportation, school supplies, leisure, and more.

### 3. SET BUDGET LIMITS
Define budget targets for each category.

### 4. MONITOR & ANALYZE
Track spending patterns through charts and summaries.

### 5. FORECAST
Predict when the budget may run low based on current spending.

### 6. ALERT
Receive notifications before exceeding budget limits or when funds are about to run out.

### 7. IMPROVE
Use weekly summaries to identify better spending habits and make improved financial decisions.

## 👥 Who Benefits?

The proposed solution can benefit:

- College students managing allowances and expenses
- Working students with variable income
- Recent graduates adjusting to independent living

## ✨ Expected Impact

The expected impact of the proposed solution includes:

- Better Financial Awareness
- Better Spending Habits
- Better Budgeting Skills
- Better Financial Decisions

## 🎨 Design Choices

Blue was used as the dominant color in the infographic to create a clear, organized, and consistent visual appearance.

This also reflected my initial preference for incorporating blue into my designs because, as an Atenean, I initially wanted to establish a visual identity inspired by the Ateneo community.

Icons and illustrations were used throughout the infographic to simplify information and make the content easier to understand.

The information was also divided into clear sections:

- The Problem
- Supporting Information
- Proposed Solution
- How It Works
- Who Benefits
- Expected Impact

This structure helps guide the audience through the infographic in a logical and organized way.
